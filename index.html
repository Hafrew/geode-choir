Crystals 1 / 8

Echoes that strike a crystal ring it for hum. New crystals find their own spot; drag one to move it, or drop it on a twin to fuse them into the next stone. After your first descent, two zones appear at the foot of the cave while you drag: Fuse joins the stone with any twin, and Crush breaks it up.

Tuning

Wonders

Every crystal slowly cracks from ringing and sheds shards; bigger stones shed more. Wonders place themselves and can be dragged like crystals. They are lost when you descend.

Attunement

Tune a whole kind of stone with shards. Each level doubles what it rings for.

The Settling

Strata

Fossils are what you carry down when you descend. Strata never reset until a Heartstone is kindled.

Patient Choir

Buys one affordable upgrade per enabled category every half-second while you are in the cave. Uses normal shop unlocks and limits. Categories and reserves are kept through every reset.

The Sunvein

Illuminations

Glowworms hang from the ceiling and eat any echo that touches their threads, turning it into lumen. Spend it on light, and on hands that play the cave for you.

Bells 1 / 8

A bell rings when a ripple reaches it. When two ripples reach the same bell at almost the same moment, they cross and it rings far harder. Drag bells to move them; drop one on a twin to fuse, or into the corner Fuse/Crush zones. Fuse needs a twin and stops at Abyssal; Crush removes the bell without a refund.

The Choir Above

The horn rack

Every horn found brings ivory; Ivory Echo increases that reward. Salvage spare horns for more. Spend ivory on horn upgrades. Rarity Weaving shifts new horns toward higher rarities; fossils or fathoms can call more horns.

Worn horns 0 / 1

Only the horns you wear count, in both worlds. Click a horn to look at it.

First voices

Primordial horns have their own slots. Duplicate traits use the strongest worn copy.

A listening rack

Compares stats and traits across both racks. Balanced values both worlds equally. Turn off auto-equip to change worn horns manually.

Auto-salvage new spare horns

Equipped horns are kept. Filters affect new finds only. At 30 horns, auto-equip makes room for an upgrade by salvaging the weakest spare; Gilded spares stay protected unless included above. If there is no room, the new horn becomes ivory.

The horn call

The odds

The rarity is spun first. Then you play one note for each stat on the horn: how well you hit it sets that stat, from half to one and a half times its usual value.

Collection 0 / 48

One design for every shape and rarity, and a gilded one for each shape. Finding a horn, even one you salvage at once, adds it here.
