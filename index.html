Crystals 1 / 8

Echoes that strike a crystal ring it for hum. New crystals find their own spot; drag one to move it, or drop it on a twin to fuse them into the next stone.

Voices

Tuning

Wonders

Every crystal slowly cracks from ringing and sheds shards; bigger stones shed more. Wonders place themselves and can be dragged like crystals. They are lost when you descend.

Attunement

Tune a whole kind of stone with shards. Each level doubles what it rings for.

Strata

Fossils are what you carry down when you descend. Strata never reset until a Heartstone is kindled.

Illuminations

Glowworms hang from the ceiling and eat any echo that touches their threads, turning it into lumen. Spend it on light, and on hands that play the cave for you.

The Heartstone

Bells 1 / 8

A bell rings when a ripple reaches it. When two ripples reach the same bell at almost the same moment, they cross and it rings far harder. Drag bells to move them; drop one on a twin to fuse.

Voices

Tuning

Oysters

Oysters open after enough ripples wash over them and give up a pearl.

Pearl works

Bell casting

Recast a whole kind of bell with pearls. Each level doubles what it rings for.

The Deep

Worn horns 0 / 1

Only the horns you equip count, in both worlds. Horns turn up on their own over time, and when you descend, sound the depths or kindle a Heartstone. Each is random. Rarer horns have bigger, and sometimes multiplying, bonuses.

The horn rack

Salvage horns you don't want for ivory. Chances per horn: Common 58%, Rare 27%, Epic 10%, Legendary 4%, Mythic 1%.
