Crystals 1 / 8

Echoes that strike a crystal ring it for hum. New crystals find their own spot; drag one to move it, or drop it on a twin to fuse them into the next stone. After your first descent, two zones appear at the foot of the cave while you drag: Fuse joins the stone with any twin, and Crush breaks it up.

Tuning

Wonders

Every crystal slowly cracks from ringing and sheds shards; bigger stones shed more. Wonders place themselves and can be dragged like crystals. They are lost when you descend.

Attunement

Tune a whole kind of stone with shards. Each level doubles what it rings for.

The Settling

Strata

Fossils are what you carry down when you descend. Strata never reset until a Heartstone is kindled.

Illuminations

Glowworms hang from the ceiling and eat any echo that touches their threads, turning it into lumen. Spend it on light, and on hands that play the cave for you.

Bells 1 / 8

A bell rings when a ripple reaches it. When two ripples reach the same bell at almost the same moment, they cross and it rings far harder. Drag bells to move them; drop one on a twin to fuse.
